Ye Portal Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

2015 se 2025 tak — 11 saal ke bharose ka digital roop.

01. Signup

Naam, mobile number, class aur ek face photo dekar registration hota hai. Turant ek unique Username & Password generate hota hai jo sirf tumhe milta hai.

02. WhatsApp Confirmation

Signup ke baad WhatsApp khulta hai with tumhari details pre-filled — tumhe bas apni photo attach karke Send dabana hota hai. Ye confirmation ke liye hai.

03. Content Library

Login karne ke baad tumhe Photos, Videos, PDFs aur PPTs milte hain — sab kuch sirf website ke andar dekha ja sakta hai. Koi download button nahi hai, aur right-click bhi disable hai.

04. Like & Comment

Har content pe tum like aur comment kar sakte ho — sab students ko wo dikhta hai, jaise ek chhoti si community.

05. Masti Batch Courses

Course dekh sakte ho aur WhatsApp ke through enroll kar sakte ho.

06. Privacy

Tumhara data (naam, mobile, photo) secure database mein store hota hai, sirf portal chalane ke liye use hota hai.
